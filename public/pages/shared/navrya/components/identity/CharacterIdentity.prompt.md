# CharacterIdentity

Character title, username line and quote — the identity column of the header.

```jsx
<CharacterIdentity character="master" name="RAYAN LAND" handle="@rayanland"
  quote="Cycles repeat. The Master remembers." />
```

- Title is Cinzel SemiBold 34/42 in the character accent; username 14/20; quote 13/20 italic in muted text.
- Titles are always the definite form — THE HUNTER, THE MARKET ENGINEER.
